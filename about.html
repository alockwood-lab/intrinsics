What is Intrinsics

Intrinsics is a free stock analysis tool that breaks down any US-listed company's financial health into simple, color-coded grades. No jargon, no paywall, no login required. Just search a ticker and get an instant read on whether the fundamentals are strong, shaky, or somewhere in between.

How it works

Every stock is evaluated across 6 key financial ratios, each graded against sector-specific benchmarks. A tech company is measured against tech peers, not utilities. The result is a clear, honest snapshot of financial health.

  • Current Ratio — Can the company pay its short-term bills?
  • Debt to Equity — How much does it rely on borrowed money?
  • Asset Turnover — How efficiently does it use what it owns?
  • Operating Margin — How profitable is the core business?
  • P/E Ratio — Are investors paying a fair price?
  • Revenue Growth — Is the top line growing or shrinking?
